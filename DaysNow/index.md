# DaysNow

Simple Countdowns of Days

DaysNow helps you keep track of dates that matter on iPhone and Mac. Add a title and a target date up to 100 years away, search your countdowns, or sort by days remaining or title.

Small and medium widgets show upcoming countdowns. Optional local notifications are scheduled for 9:00 AM on the target day. Countdown data can sync through your private iCloud storage, and the app follows your device’s Light or Dark appearance.

DaysNow Free includes up to 2 active and 2 inactive countdowns. The one-time DaysNow Full purchase unlocks unlimited active and inactive countdowns.
