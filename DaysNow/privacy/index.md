# DaysNow Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 6 October 2026**

## Information collected by the developer

The developer does not receive your countdown content, notification content, or app preferences through DaysNow.

DaysNow does not include third-party advertising, analytics, tracking, crash-reporting, or data-collection services.

## Countdown data

DaysNow stores countdowns locally on your device.

If iCloud access is enabled and available, DaysNow can use your private iCloud storage to keep countdowns synchronised across supported Apple devices.

Private CloudKit data is managed by Apple and is not available to the developer through the CloudKit developer portal.

The app and its widget read the same local App Group store on each device. The app manages CloudKit synchronisation; the widget does not use an independent countdown dataset or CloudKit connection. Widget refresh timing is controlled by Apple.

## Preferences

DaysNow preferences and settings are stored locally on your device.

They are not sent to the developer.

## Notifications

DaysNow schedules notifications locally on your device.

The developer does not send notification content to you from a developer-operated server.

Notification delivery is controlled by Apple’s operating system and may be delayed or prevented by notification permissions, Focus settings, device state, or other system conditions.

## Purchases

DaysNow Full purchases and restorations are processed by Apple through the App Store and StoreKit.

The developer does not operate a separate payment system and does not receive your payment-card information.

Apple may provide the developer with App Store sales and purchase reporting as part of its developer services.

## Apple analytics and diagnostics

Apple may provide the developer with aggregated App Store performance information and, where users choose to share it with app developers, crash, diagnostic, and usage information.

This information is provided by Apple and is not collected by DaysNow through a developer-operated analytics or tracking service.

## Tracking

DaysNow does not track you across apps or websites.

DaysNow does not use tracking domains or advertising identifiers.

## Third parties

DaysNow does not include third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Apple services used by DaysNow, including iCloud, StoreKit, notifications, and App Store services, are governed by Apple’s own privacy practices.

## Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if DaysNow’s features or data practices change.

The latest version will be available on this page.

## Website and support correspondence

These public pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub handles website requests under its own privacy practices. DaysNow does not add advertising or analytics to these pages.

If you email support, the developer receives the message and contact information you choose to send. Do not include passwords, payment information, or private countdown content.

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or DaysNow’s privacy practices, contact:
