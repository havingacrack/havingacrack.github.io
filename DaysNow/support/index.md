# DaysNow Support

Need help with DaysNow, want to report a problem, or have a suggestion?

Please contact:

**Email:** [daysnowapp@outlook.com](mailto:daysnowapp@outlook.com)

Include your app version, whether you use iPhone or Mac, and what happened. Do not send passwords, payment details, or private countdown content.

## About DaysNow

DaysNow is a deliberately simple countdown-of-days app for iPhone and Mac.

Add a title and target date up to 100 years away, and DaysNow takes care of the countdown.

## Common Questions

### How many countdowns can I use?

**DaysNow Free** supports up to:

- 2 active countdowns
- 2 inactive countdowns

**DaysNow Full** is a one-time purchase that unlocks unlimited active and inactive countdowns on supported devices, currently iPhone and Mac.

Today and future target dates are active; past target dates are inactive. If either Free category already has 2 countdowns, tapping **+** shows the Free Limit prompt before the editor opens. Save-time checks also protect the limits if data or entitlement changes while editing.

If a Free dataset exceeds the limits, DaysNow asks you to upgrade, restore an existing purchase, or delete excessive countdowns. Delete Excessive keeps the 2 most imminent active and 2 most recent inactive countdowns; review the confirmation before deleting.

Deletion also propagates through iCloud to your other supported devices. If countdowns tie on the retention boundary, automatic deletion is unavailable; restore or upgrade to continue without choosing between tied records.

### How do notifications work?

Enable **Notifications** in DaysNow Settings and allow notifications in the operating system. DaysNow schedules a local notification for **9:00 AM on a countdown’s target day**, provided that time is still in the future. A countdown added after 9:00 AM on its target day does not receive a retrospective notification.

Notification delivery is controlled by the operating system and may be delayed or prevented by notification permissions, Focus, device state, or other system conditions.

DaysNow is a general-purpose utility, not a guaranteed reminder service. Verify important dates independently.

### How does iCloud sync work?

DaysNow stores countdowns locally and can use your private iCloud database to keep them synchronised across supported Apple devices when iCloud is available.

iCloud synchronisation timing and availability are managed by Apple and may not be immediate.

### How do I restore DaysNow Full?

Open **DaysNow Settings** and choose **Restore** in the inline **DaysNow — Free/Full** section. There is no separate DaysNow Full screen.

Restore asks Apple to check for an existing DaysNow Full purchase associated with your Apple Account.

To buy Full, choose **Upgrade** in that same Settings section while Free is active. Apple presents the purchase and current price. Once Full is verified, Settings shows **DaysNow — Full**, Upgrade disappears, and Restore remains available.

If Restore reports that no current purchase was found, check that you are using the Apple Account associated with the purchase. If Apple reports a connection or account error, try Restore again when the service is available; an error is not confirmation that a purchase has been lost.

### How do widgets work?

Add the small or medium DaysNow widget using your iPhone Home Screen widget gallery or the Mac’s Edit Widgets gallery. On Mac, choose **On This Mac** for the native Mac widget rather than a widget labelled **From iPhone**.

Widgets show active countdowns from the same local shared store as the app. The app handles iCloud sync; the widget does not run its own iCloud sync. Apple controls widget refresh scheduling, so app changes and iCloud arrivals may not appear immediately. Open DaysNow to check the current data. With no active countdowns, the widget shows **No Countdowns**.

### How do I change appearance?

DaysNow follows the system Light or Dark appearance; there is no in-app appearance selector. On iPhone, change **Settings → Display & Brightness → Appearance**. Home Screen icon customisation is a separate setting and does not change the app’s system appearance.

### What does DaysNow Full unlock?

DaysNow Full unlocks unlimited active and inactive countdowns on supported devices, currently iPhone and Mac.

### Does DaysNow require an account?

DaysNow does not require a DaysNow account or login.

Apple services such as iCloud sync and App Store purchases may use your Apple Account.
