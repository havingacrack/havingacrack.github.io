MatchWow Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 October 2026

HomeSupportPrivacy Policy

MatchWow is an offline-capable iPhone puzzle game. It does not require a custom MatchWow account. Apple's Game Centre is optional and has separate data processing, described below.

Local gameplay data and preferences

MatchWow stores your current Endless and Challenge games on your iPhone so you can resume them. Saved information includes jewel identities and boards, puzzle seeds and random-generator state, objectives, moves, score, frost, results and the information needed for an exact retry. The app also stores preferences such as appearance, sound, haptics and reduced motion, together with local achievement statistics and reporting checkpoints.

The developer does not operate a server that receives your saved boards, puzzle files or preferences. MatchWow does not provide cross-device game synchronisation. Device backups, offloading and restoration are controlled by iOS and your Apple settings.

Optional Apple Game Centre

MatchWow uses Apple's GameKit framework for optional Game Centre authentication, achievements and the native dashboard. If you use these features, Apple processes your Game Centre account and game activity under its own privacy practices. MatchWow uses the game-scoped player identifier provided by Apple to keep local account progress separate and reconcile achievements safely.

Achievement identifiers and completion percentages are reported to Game Centre when an authenticated connection is available. Local statistics, account-specific progress and pending reporting information are stored on the device to support offline play and avoid duplicate reporting. Game Centre associates online achievements with your Game Centre player account. Apple controls its native player information and dashboard.

You can play without signing in to Game Centre. Core gameplay and local saves remain available when Apple's service is unavailable or you are offline. MatchWow does not currently submit competitive leaderboard scores.

See Apple's Game Centre privacy information for Apple's processing and account controls.

Network services, advertising and tracking

Core puzzle generation and gameplay take place on your iPhone. Optional Game Centre features communicate with Apple's services. Opening a Support or Privacy Policy link also requires a connection to the relevant website.

MatchWow contains no advertising, third-party analytics, tracking SDKs or developer-operated crash-reporting service. It does not use an advertising identifier or track you across other companies' apps or websites. Apple may provide App Store reporting and diagnostics according to your Apple settings and Apple's privacy practices; that is separate from any analytics system operated by MatchWow's developer.

Sharing and support enquiries

The developer does not receive your saved games through a MatchWow backend. Optional achievement information is transferred to Apple's Game Centre services as described above. There is no developer-run social account system or gameplay-data upload service.

If you email support, the developer receives your email address and the message or attachments you choose to send, through the support email service. These are used to respond to your enquiry. Do not include passwords, payment information or private account details. Contact us if you need assistance with a privacy or support-correspondence request.

GitHub hosts these information pages. They contain no added JavaScript, analytics, tracking cookies or remote fonts. GitHub may process technical information about website requests under its own privacy statement.

Retention and deletion

Local games remain on the device so you can resume them. Starting a new game replaces the corresponding saved game; replacing a game that is still in progress asks for confirmation. Retrying resets that Challenge attempt while keeping the same puzzle specification. MatchWow does not provide a general in-app erase-all-data control.

Deleting the app removes its local data from that installation. Offloading, backups and later restoration can behave differently according to your Apple settings. Deleting MatchWow does not delete achievement records maintained separately by Game Centre. Apple manages those records and its account/privacy controls; contact Apple or use its privacy resources for help with Apple-held data.

Policy updates and privacy contact

This policy will be updated if MatchWow's data practices change. The effective date above identifies the current version.

For privacy questions or assistance, email daysnowapp@outlook.com.
