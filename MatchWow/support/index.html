MatchWow is a native iOS match-3 puzzle game featuring colourful glass jewels, cascading matches and two gameplay modes. There is no timer, so you can take your time.

How to play

Swipe a jewel towards an adjacent neighbour, or select a jewel and then select its neighbour. A valid swap makes a horizontal or vertical line of three or more matching jewels. Matched jewels clear, the jewels above fall into place, and new jewels enter from the top. New matches can create extra cascade waves.

Use the sparkle button beside the instruction card for a suggested legal move. It highlights the move but does not play it for you or use a Challenge move.

Welcome screen

The Welcome screen has five cards:

  1. Resume: restores a saved game. If both Endless and Challenge have saved games, choose which one to resume.
  2. New Game: start Endless or choose Easy, Normal, Hard or Get Outta Here Challenge.
  3. Endless Mode: start or resume Endless play.
  4. Game Centre: view optional achievements and the Apple player interface.
  5. Help & Settings: read how to play and adjust sound, haptics, appearance and accessibility settings.

Games are saved on this iPhone. Endless and Challenge sessions are stored separately. Starting a game that would replace an unfinished session asks you to confirm.

Gameplay toolbar

The five actions at the bottom of the gameplay screen are:

  1. Home: saves your current game and returns to Welcome. It does not end or delete the game.
  2. Start Over: restarts the current game. You are asked to confirm if this would discard unfinished progress.
  3. New Game: starts Endless or opens the Challenge difficulty choices. Replacing an unfinished game asks you to confirm.
  4. Game Centre: opens the optional Apple achievements and player interface.
  5. Help & Settings: opens play instructions and settings.

Endless Mode

Endless is continuing match-3 gameplay with the six classic jewel varieties and no Challenge move limit. There is no timer. Your game is saved locally so you can return to it later. If a settled board has no legal swap, MatchWow refreshes the board.

Challenge Mode

Objectives can include collecting a named jewel, clearing frosted cells, earning points or creating extra cascade waves. The number of objectives increases with difficulty. Only a valid matching swap uses a move. All cascades from that move count before the game checks your progress.

When you complete a Challenge, the next difficulty starts automatically: Easy, Normal, Hard, Get Outta Here, then back to Easy. Your current Challenge is saved so you can resume it later.

On the results screen, Retry This Puzzle lets you try the same Challenge again. Try a Fresh Puzzle creates another Challenge at the same difficulty.

Appearance

Choose Light, Dark or Follow System in Help & Settings. Light uses the Classic Dark gameplay artwork. Dark uses the Moonlit Forest artwork. Follow System uses the appearance selected on your iPhone.

Optional Game Centre

Game Centre provides optional achievements and Apple's native player interface. Open it from the Welcome screen or gameplay toolbar. Local games remain playable without signing in, and achievements can update when Apple's service and an internet connection are available. MatchWow does not submit Endless totals to a leaderboard.

Troubleshooting

  • Resume a game: use Resume on the Welcome screen. If both Endless and Challenge are saved, select the one you want to continue.
  • Restart a game: choose Start Over in the gameplay toolbar. If unfinished progress would be lost, confirm before the restart.
  • Try a different Challenge: choose New Game in the gameplay toolbar, then select a difficulty. Replacing unfinished progress asks for confirmation.
  • Sound or haptics: check the Sound and Haptics preferences in Help & Settings. Sound also respects the iPhone's silent-mode behaviour. Haptic availability depends on the device.
  • Appearance and accessibility: use the Appearance and Reduce Motion preferences in Help & Settings, together with iPhone text size, VoiceOver, Reduce Motion and Dim Flashing Lights settings. Large text may require scrolling. Tiles announce their jewel, row, column and frosted state; adjacent-swap actions are available through VoiceOver Actions.
  • Achievements not updating: check your internet connection and Game Centre sign-in, then reopen Game Centre. Eligible local progress is retained for later reconciliation. Contact support if the problem continues.
  • Unexpected behaviour: close and reopen MatchWow, then choose Resume. Keep your app and iOS up to date. Avoid deleting the app to troubleshoot a save problem, because deleting it can remove local progress.
