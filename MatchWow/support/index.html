MatchWow is a native iOS match-3 puzzle game featuring colourful glass jewels, cascading matches and two gameplay modes. There is no timer, so you can take your time.

How to play

Swipe a jewel towards an adjacent neighbour, or select a jewel and then select its neighbour. A valid swap makes a horizontal or vertical line of three or more identical jewels. Matched jewels clear, the jewels above fall into place, and new jewels enter from the top. Any new matches create another cascade wave.

A matched jewel is worth 10 points. Each straight matching run earns 20 extra points for each jewel beyond three. The wave total is multiplied by its position in the cascade: 1 for the first wave, 2 for the second, 3 for the third, and so on. A single straight run of three, four or five scores 30, 60 or 90 points in the first wave. Intersecting jewels are removed and counted once, while each long run earns its own length bonus.

Hint, the circular question mark beside Pause, identifies a legal swap and selects its first jewel. It does not play the move or use a move allowance, and it is not necessarily the best choice for your objectives. Pause opens the saved-game controls and help.

Endless Mode

Play with the original six jewel varieties, unlimited moves and no move limit or timer. Keep matching and building your score. If a settled board has no legal swap, MatchWow automatically refreshes it. Your current Endless game is saved locally so you can resume it later.

Objectives can include collecting a named jewel, clearing frosted cells, earning points and producing extra cascade waves. Easy has one goal; Normal has two, Hard three and Get Outta Here four. Only a valid matching swap uses a Challenge move. All waves from that move count before the result is assessed.

Retry This Puzzle restores the same starting board, jewels, goals, move allowance and incoming jewel sequence. Identical moves reproduce the same cascades. Try a Fresh Puzzle generates a new Challenge at the same difficulty. The jewel designs can vary between harder Challenges, but stay fixed throughout each puzzle.

A Challenge saved before the progressive-jewel update keeps its original six jewels and rules. Finish it or deliberately start a fresh Challenge to use the new settings. Replacing a game that is still in progress asks for confirmation.

Optional Game Centre

Game Centre provides optional achievements and Apple's native player interface. Open Game Centre from the main menu or Pause screen. Ordinary gameplay does not require Game Centre sign-in, and local games remain playable offline. Online achievement updates require Apple's service and an internet connection. MatchWow does not submit unlimited Endless totals to a leaderboard.

Troubleshooting

  • Resume a game: return to the main menu and use the appropriate Resume option. Endless and Challenge progress are stored separately on this iPhone; there is no cross-device game sync.
  • Restart a Challenge: use Retry This Puzzle on the results screen. To replace an unfinished puzzle, use New Game in Pause and read the confirmation carefully.
  • Sound or haptics: check the app's Sound and Haptics preferences. Sound also respects the iPhone's silent-mode behaviour. Haptic availability depends on the device.
  • Appearance and accessibility: use the app's appearance and Reduce Motion preferences, together with iPhone text-size, VoiceOver, Reduce Motion and Dim Flashing Lights settings. Large text may require scrolling. Tiles announce their jewel, row, column and frosted state; adjacent-swap actions are available through VoiceOver Actions.
  • Achievements not updating: check your internet connection and Game Centre sign-in, then reopen Game Centre. Eligible local progress is retained for later reconciliation. Contact support if the problem continues.
  • Unexpected behaviour: close and reopen MatchWow, then try Resume. Keep your app and iOS up to date. Avoid deleting the app to troubleshoot a save problem, because deleting it can remove local progress.
