# StuffNow

**Know what you own and where you put it.**

StuffNow is a household inventory app for Mac. Record item, purchase and warranty details; organise by location, category and tags; search your inventory; and create printable reports.

Your inventory is stored locally on your Mac. CSV import/export, PDF reports, manual backup files and automatic local backups help you move and protect your data. StuffNow requires no developer-operated account and has no in-app purchase or subscription flow.
