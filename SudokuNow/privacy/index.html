SudokuNow Privacy Policy

SudokuNow is an on-device Sudoku game. It requires no account and contains no ads, analytics or tracking.

Your game stays on your device

Puzzles are generated locally. Your active puzzle and game progress are stored locally on your device so you can resume playing. SudokuNow does not upload that state or collect it through cloud or network services.

External links and support

Opening the app’s Privacy Policy or Support link takes you to a website. These pages contain no analytics, cookies, trackers, remote fonts or JavaScript added by SudokuNow. GitHub hosts the pages and handles website requests under its own privacy practices.
