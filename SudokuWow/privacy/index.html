SudokuWow Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

SudokuWow is an on-device Sudoku game. It requires no account and contains no ads, analytics or tracking.

Your game stays on your device

Puzzles are generated locally. Your active puzzle and game progress are stored locally on your device so you can resume playing. SudokuWow does not upload that state or collect it through cloud or network services.

The app does not provide cross-device game sync. Replacing or ending the current game removes that saved progress. Device backups and other operating-system services are governed by your Apple settings.

Apple services

Downloads and any App Store payments are handled by Apple. SudokuWow has no in-app purchase or subscription implementation; the developer does not receive payment-card details. Apple may provide App Store reporting and, if you choose to share diagnostics with developers, crash or usage information under Apple’s privacy practices. SudokuWow has no developer-operated analytics or crash-reporting service.

External links and support

Opening the app’s Privacy Policy or Support link takes you to a website. These pages contain no analytics, cookies, trackers, remote fonts or JavaScript added by SudokuWow. GitHub hosts the pages and handles website requests under its own privacy practices.

If you email support, the developer receives the message and contact information you choose to send. Do not include passwords or private account information.

Changes and contact

This page will be updated if SudokuWow’s data practices change. For privacy questions, email daysnowapp@outlook.com.
